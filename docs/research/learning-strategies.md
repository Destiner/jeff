# R5: learn decisions, not just synthetic-label agreement

**Recommendation:** propose **one bounded supervised Qwen0.5B decision-learning run**, keeping its frozen comparator, encoders and evaluation boundaries intact. Train the existing caller-choice objective, not a language/rationale generator. If local training cannot be made feasible within the remaining window, the **one contingent alternative** is a saved-output-only, group-cross-fitted temperature diagnostic—not a claim to repair reasoning. Neither is authorized or launched by this memo.

R5 is a time-boxed research synthesis, not another backbone/LR survey or a review gate. [Source detail and limitations](../../experiments/research/learning-strategies-evidence.md) distinguish published findings, mathematical deductions and our proposed experiment. Existing [R1](recommendation.md)/[R4](m3-pilot-probe-diagnosis.md) remain historical evidence.

## Primary: one fixed decoder-learning comparison

**Hypothesis:** existing Qwen representations can be adapted to the choice task using authorized training labels, improving broad probability quality and order robustness without erasing its useful intent/probe signal. This is a recipe-level hypothesis, **not** an architecture, permutation-only, or causal-mechanism ablation.

**Intervention/control specification, proposed before any future outcomes:**

- Start from P3's pinned `Qwen/Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct` revision `7ae557604adf67be50417f59c2c2f167def9a775`, not a trained encoder or a different model. Keep P3's official chat template, instruction, A–F boundary checks, option-ID mapping, temperature1, cap1024/no truncation, FP32/MPS and four threads. Preserve P3 unchanged as the **untrained control**; M4 encoders remain contextual comparators, not a matched architecture control.
- Use the exact frozen v2 **TRAIN** release only:1,500 decisions, unchanged labels/group membership. [M4's release receipt](../model/m4-v2-plan.md#released-data-recipe-frozen-before-training) pins `artifacts/data/data-pilot-v2/manifest.json` SHA256 `d97e965b7332ec8ae933c1f5bbf261816fb0db47b2fc06b347b053aabd2066ac`; recheck its TRAIN shard hashes before execution, not R3's superseded candidate hash. No D3/probe/validation/final examples, paraphrases, teacher probabilities or new rationale/data generation.
- Proposed single recipe: full-parameter AdamW, LR`2e-5`, betas`(.9,.999)`, epsilon`1e-8`, weight decay`.01`, gradient clipping1; one epoch, microbatch1, accumulation4 (**375 updates**), seed42, no LR/epoch sweep. Grouped CE over the allowed answer-symbol logits, not full-vocabulary language loss. One seeded random option permutation per presentation with the target remapped by identity; materialize/hash that schedule before preflight and use it unchanged. Do not multiply presentations by all permutations. Retain the final checkpoint, not the best checkpoint selected on development.
- Preflight every TRAIN prompt/boundary in that exact permutation schedule before loading; overflow or incompatible tokenization blocks this recipe rather than silently truncating/filtering. A Model-owned train-only capacity check must precede commitment to the run; it is not an invitation for Research to run one. Any disposable optimizer-allocation step must be discarded, with model/optimizer/RNG state restored to the pinned start; account for its cost, never add an uncounted update to the375-update run. Freeze any implementation-driven amendment **before** learning outcomes and report it as a new recipe. No automatic LoRA/dependency/cloud fallback.
- The job is decision-only. Restricting CE to allowed symbols leaves other vocabulary behavior unconstrained; no preservation of chat/generation ability is claimed. Avoid adding KL anchors, scratchpads, temperatures, new prompt templates or weighted-source sampling to this first contrast.

**Observation and interpretation:** Evaluation can reuse E5's exact inherited60/new240/probe24-over12 reporting, original/reversed pairs, full coverage, NLL/Brier, family/source-unit macros, flip/TV rates and both-order agreement. Report all components, not a new post-outcome promotion score. A convincing positive signal requires lower proper losses across both orders and both broad strata, reduced new-rule flips with more both-order successes, and preservation of intent/probe agreement—not just an original-order accuracy gain. Report paired group contributions and every regression; tiny differences and one seed do not establish significance. No numerical acceptance threshold or default promotion is introduced here.

Within the same authorized run, Model should record deterministic train-fit summaries before/after on the same predeclared rendering schedule (train agreement, CE and answer-position distribution); do not add a separate search. Poor train fit leaves input/labels/optimization/capacity unresolved. Good fit with worse development performance implicates transfer/exposure mismatch without identifying its cause. Fewer flips without more correct pairs can simply mean **consistently wrong**. Better NLL without ranking gains is a probability improvement, not rule acquisition. Failure would reject this bounded recipe, not all decoders. Qwen has **no D3 baseline** in P3; do not pool D3 into this contrast or infer unseen-composition success.

**Feasibility boundary:**494M parameters ×16bytes for FP32 weights+gradients+two Adam moments ≈**7.90GB (7.36GiB)** before activations, buffers, allocator workspace and extra copies. This is arithmetic, not a measured peak or24GiB fit guarantee. P3's2.53GB RSS/2.77GB observed MPS-driver figure is inference-only and not additive. Budget at most one explicitly scheduled45-minute training slot as a proposed cap, not a runtime prediction; measure a train-only backward/optimizer footprint and stop if headroom or deadline is inadequate. No spending is proposed. Dependency or allocation changes belong to lead/Model, not this memo.

## One contingent alternative: saved-output probability-scale diagnostic

**Trigger:** primary training is infeasible **before execution** because of local memory, implementation readiness or remaining time. This is an alternative, not a post-failure rescue arm.

Evaluation uses the **frozen Qwen E5 outputs only**, comparing T=1 with one positive global temperature fit on existing non-final development groups. Preassign three source-disjoint folds, keeping the broadest known related source/template units and every original/reversed pair together; if those units cannot support folds across families, do not fit. Average the two orders per decision, decisions within the declared source unit, then units equally. Fit this source-macro NLL on two folds over the41-point grid `T=10^(-1+j/20), j=0…40` (includes1); break exact objective ties by closeness to1, then lowerT. Evaluate the third fold, then rotate. Keep probes and D3 out of fitting, no per-family/position models, no threshold/prompt search. Existing probabilities permit `q_i ∝ p_i^(1/T)` only with disclosed handling of zeros/rounding. Report per-fold T, boundary hits, coverage and paired out-of-fold NLL/Brier versus T=1, separately by inherited/new/family.

Stable out-of-fold improvement in both proper scores would support a **probability-scale** explanation for part of the loss. Unstable T, subgroup regressions or no improvement reject that limited explanation. Within-case ranks and reversal choices cannot improve under positive T. Correlated and repeatedly consulted development data remain exploratory even after cross-fitting; this is neither independent calibration certification nor a deployable abstention threshold. No fitted value becomes the default automatically.

## Boundaries that remain true

Probabilities mean normalized scores over **these supplied alternatives**, not guaranteed real-world correctness. A missing correct option still receives total mass1 elsewhere; overlapping choices, ambiguity and model ignorance are distinct. Proper CE/Brier do not create trustworthy labels or source diversity. Temperature does not repair reasoning, and an abstention policy needs explicit action costs and independent reliability evidence. **No new run, dataset, teacher call, paid allocation, model promotion or review gate was created by R5.**
