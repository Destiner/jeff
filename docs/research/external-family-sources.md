# R8: three public source families for D8/E13

**Recommend original COPA, WinoGrande 1.1 debiased, and a SocialIQA prerequisite-only slice.** These provide native choices and source labels for three operational task families absent from the recorded Jeff fine-tuning families. They are **not three independent cognitive abilities**, contamination-free data, or proof of arbitrary generalization. HellaSwag is not recommended for this exercise.

This is a source/selection recommendation, not a dataset release or model-call grant. Data/Evaluation agree and freeze the exact compatible conversion and grouping under the lead's D8/E13 authority. Aim for100 exploration +100 locally sealed confirmation per family; actual eligible counts remain unmeasured here. A smaller or two-family defensible population is preferable to widening ambiguous eligibility to reach600.
