## Operational definitions

- **Workspace:** Writing desk drafts/edits prose, including scripts; Number sheet organizes/calculates/compares quantities; Visual canvas creates/arranges visual compositions; Schedule planner arranges calendar commitments and availability. Classify the requested output/action, not incidental topic words. Calculating a duration is different from arranging a timed commitment; layout of approved text is different from rewriting it.
- **Direct sentiment:** Positive expresses meaningful favorable assessment without meaningful criticism; Negative the reverse; Neutral factual information/questions without evaluation; Mixed substantive favorable and unfavorable assessments of the focal experience. Courtesy alone does not make a complaint mixed. This labels expressed sentiment, not an appropriate reaction or inferred objective quality; emojis are not target tokens.
- **Tickets:** Bug reports an observed unintended malfunction; Feature requests new/changed capability without asserting a malfunction; How-to requests guidance for existing capability; Feedback evaluates without a defect report, capability request or usage question. Tone is not intent. Unresolved compound intents require clarification, not forced labels.

Each fresh domain has 20 cases/class, ten/class in concise-label format and ten/class with descriptive options, and five/class/target slot. Final descriptive option banks occur with every target class, rather than revealing it. Ticket tone strata have five/class each: appreciative, frustrated, matter-of-fact and polite courtesy; each stratum occurs in both formats. These counts describe construction, not demonstrated model robustness.
