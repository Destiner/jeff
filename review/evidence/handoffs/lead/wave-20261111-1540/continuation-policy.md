# Owner continuation after delivery

The user wants fewer manual restarts and better use of spare capacity. The policy in AGENTS.md / docs/owner-lifecycle.md is being updated accordingly.

Deliver each assignment promptly through the mailbox with artifacts, disposition and main commit; do not delay useful results for an idle window. Then stay in the active owner turn for up to30 idle minutes, checking the canonical inbox about once a minute and at normal work boundaries. Continue a queued authorized task or accept a bounded in-scope peer request without waiting for a user relay or routine lead approval.

Only meaningful accepted work resets the idle window; acknowledgements, status notices, duplicate requests or polls do not. Do not reopen completed tasks, invent work, launch unallocated ML jobs, enlarge scope, or spend merely to stay active. Known active dependencies use their explicit expected time and task limits; no indefinite waiting.

Send final chat when actually parking or blocked beyond the bounded window. You may give one short commentary line that delivery is complete and state the availability cutoff. After parking, manual wake-up is still required; do not promise background monitoring.

Experiment windows are unchanged: stop D4/I4 observations at their original deadline, even if you remain briefly available for new messages. Explicit park-now/ownership-transfer/user-stop directions override continuation. Before parking, check once more and record pending work, active-job handoff and availability state. Never silently abandon a job you supervise.

This changes owner behavior, not mailbox software. No watcher, service, scheduler, automatic wake-up, shell-startup edit or new dependency is requested. Existing no-tests/no-lint rules and scope/compute/budget boundaries remain.
